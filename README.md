Working as an intern for MIRRA; Methods of Irrigation and Agriculture, in their office in Amman, Jordan with site visits to their Climate Smart Farm in the Jordan Valley along the Jordan River Basin. My project
tasks were to creat a highly interactive page specifically for their Climate Smart Farm and to revamp any other issues that I thought could use some help. In the first 2 weeks of my 2 month internship, I created and
and added templates for a brand new blog page, that would merge all the tabs in their media center into a easy to view grid display and minimize the amount of white space throughout the website. I also created a
highly interactive farm page template with a clickable map detailing the many differnt sectors within their Climate Smart Farm. After the first 2 weeks I began to attempt the integration of these templates I created
in Replit, only to find that the system they currently use to deploy their website; WordPress, does not do a good job of integrating the full interactivity of the pages I had created using Replit. With this issue I 
began searching for integration tools, rediting some of the HTML, researching tips and tricks to using WordPress; only to find that ultimately I would not be able to integrate the 2 weeks worth of code I had just
created. I began an attempt to reconstruct the templates I had created with the limited software and plugins that WordPress has to offer. Since I could not use the hardwork I put into these templates, I decided to
upload them to my GitHub for future employers to be able to view my growth and capabilities in website design and management.
