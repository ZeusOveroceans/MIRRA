Our Farm Highlights

Discover what makes MIRRA's Climate Smart Farm a leader in sustainable agriculture and community engagement.

100% Renewable Energy

MIRRA is a Jordanian NGO established in 2007, dedicated to advancing agriculture and water sector development through research, demonstration, and strategic partnerships with national and international institutions. The Climate Smart Farm serves as a real-world platform for testing, adapting, and showcasing innovative technologies that enhance productivity and sustainability in arid agricultural regions.
